## Multilingual UX synthesis

This brief captures findings from user research sessions conducted across four language groups to inform multilingual support in the Route App.

### Research scope

- Six participants across English, Spanish, Korean, and Mandarin
- Sessions focused on transit wayfinding comprehension and Journey Card readability
- Raw session notes available in Design Agency Google Drive

### Key findings

1. Icon-heavy layouts performed better than text-heavy ones across all groups.
2. Station name transliteration was preferred over full translation for Korean and Mandarin speakers.
3. Color-coded route lines were universally understood without language dependency.
4. Estimated arrival times needed clearer formatting for non-English locales.

### Recommendations

- Prioritize icon and color affordances over translated labels for v1.
- Use transliterated station names with original script in parentheses.
- Invest in professional localization for safety-critical strings (disruptions, alerts).

### Handoff scope

Design Agency is delivering this synthesis to the PM for release planning prioritization.
